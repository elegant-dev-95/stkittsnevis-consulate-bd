The Federation

Discover Saint Kitts and Nevis

A beautiful twin-island nation in the Eastern Caribbean, Saint Kitts and Nevis is known for its rich history, vibrant culture, natural beauty and warm hospitality. It is the smallest sovereign federation in the world.

Official NameFederation of Saint Kitts and Nevis
CapitalBasseterre (Saint Kitts)
Independence19 September 1983
Official LanguageEnglish
CurrencyEastern Caribbean Dollar (XCD)
MottoCountry Above Self

Diplomatic relations with Bangladesh were established on 31 August 2020.

Location & Geography

Twin islands in the Eastern Caribbean

Saint Kitts and Nevis lies in the Caribbean Sea. The two islands are separated by a narrow channel known as The Narrows.

Culture & People

A vibrant Caribbean heritage

The people of Saint Kitts and Nevis are known as Kittitians and Nevisians. English is the official language.
